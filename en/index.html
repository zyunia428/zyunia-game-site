Guidelines

These guidelines apply to all of my games.

Let's Plays, streams and videos

Individuals and companies are free to stream and post videos without contacting me first. Spoilers, showing the ending, and monetizing through ads or tips are also allowed.

I'd appreciate it if you could include the game's URL in the description, or the store URL for paid games. If your content has spoilers, please mention that as well.

Fan works

Fan art, videos and other fan works are welcome. Selling doujinshi and merchandise is also allowed within the scope of personal doujin activities.

Please contact me in advance about use by companies or commercial use beyond the scope of doujin activities.

Requests

  • Please do not redistribute the games or their assets.
  • Please do not present your work in a way that could be mistaken for official content, or use the games to hurt others or for illegal purposes.
  • Please respect the rights of third parties and the terms of any third-party assets used in the games.

If you are unsure about anything, please contact me. These guidelines may change when necessary.
